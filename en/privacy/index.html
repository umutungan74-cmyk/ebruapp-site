2. What stays on your device

  • prayers you have ticked off, streaks, made-up prayers (qada) and learning progress
  • your name in the app, your profile picture and your settings
  • days you mark as paused in period mode: this stays on your device and is never transferred, not even to the friends feature.

This data does not reach us. You choose a profile picture yourself through the photo picker; it is stored only in the app on your device. If you delete the app, this data is gone.

3. Location and Qibla

Ebru uses your location only if you allow it, and only while the app is open. Your device uses it to calculate prayer times and the direction of the Kaaba. The coordinates are stored on your device and are not sent to us or to Supabase.

To turn coordinates into a place name, or to find a city you type in, the app uses your operating system's location service. Your coordinates or search term may be sent to Apple (iPhone) or Google (Android) for this. You can also enter your place by hand instead of allowing location access. For the mosque search, see section 10.

4. Notifications

The app schedules prayer time reminders locally on your device. Nothing is sent to us for this. Only if you use the Friends feature are there also push notifications (see section 5).

5. Friends feature (optional)

This feature is off by default. Only when you tap "I agree" on the consent screen does the app connect to the server and create an anonymous account with no email, phone number or password. Because your daily status can reveal your religious practice, we treat it as a special category of personal data.

Legal basis: your explicit consent, Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time, with effect for the future, by deleting your account.

What is stored on the server

  • a random account ID
  • the name you enter for this feature, and your colour
  • app language, time of consent, whether you have Premium, and your setting for friend notifications
  • for each prayer day, "completed" (all five prayers) or the withdrawal of that status and, only if you allow "Share how many prayers per day", the number of prayers ticked off (a number from 0 to 5), not which ones and not when
  • your progress on the learning path as one number ("station x of y"), only if you allow "Share learning progress with friends"; if you turn it off, the number is deleted from the server
  • which version of the consent you confirmed
  • friendships, shared streak, longest streak and streak protection used
  • invite codes (single use, valid for 7 days at most) and attempts to redeem a code (protection against guessing, kept for about 1 day at most)
  • greetings sent, from fixed texts only; free-text messages do not exist
  • your push token, if you allow notifications

What is never sent

Which prayer you performed and when, which texts you learned, your location and your profile picture. Both the number of prayers and your learning progress stay off until you explicitly allow them.

Who sees what

Only people you are friends with through an invite code can see your name, your colour, your completed days from the last 35 days and your shared streak. If you allow it, they also see how many prayers you ticked off today (e.g. "3/5", without times) and your learning progress (e.g. "station 5/22"). There is no public profile, no search and no leaderboard. Friends cannot see your push token or settings.

Push notifications

Friend notifications contain the name and a fixed text, for example "… completed the day." They are delivered through Expo's push service and then through Apple or Google.

Retention and deletion

Whenever you report a day, the server deletes your daily statuses and greetings older than 35 days. If you stop reporting days, the existing entries remain until you delete your account. The shared streak number remains for as long as the friendship does.

With Delete account you remove your account and all related data from the database immediately.

6. Subscriptions

Purchases and subscriptions are made through Apple's App Store. We do not receive payment details. Apple processes them under its own responsibility. The legal basis is performance of a contract, Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR.

We use RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. When it starts, the app asks RevenueCat for prices and your subscription status. RevenueCat receives a random user identifier that RevenueCat assigns itself, details of your purchases and technical device data such as app version, operating system and IP address, so that your subscription can be recognised and restored. If you use the Friends feature, the app links your purchases to the random account ID of your Friends account, so that our server can check Premium for the Friends feature with RevenueCat. RevenueCat does not receive your name, your location or your prayer data.

7. App updates

The app asks Expo's service for updates. This transfers technically necessary data such as IP address, app version, platform and a random installation identifier. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an up-to-date and secure app, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

8. Crash reports

So that we can find and fix crashes and errors, the app uses the Sentry service (Functional Software, Inc., USA) with data stored in its EU region. When an error occurs, the app sends an error report. It contains only technical information: the type and location of the error in the program code, the last steps in the app before it (for example controls tapped and network requests), app version, operating system, device model, language and time zone, and a random installation identifier. Your IP address is technically transmitted when the report is sent.

Reports do not contain a user identifier, your name, your location, your prayer data, screenshots or recordings of your use. Output from the app console and requests to the Friends server are filtered out of the reports. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an error-free and secure app, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

9. Quran recitation and translations

In the Quran reader, the app plays the recitation by Mishary Rashid Alafasy verse by verse as a stream from everyayah.com. The app downloads the German and English translations from QuranEnc.com the first time you open a surah and then keeps them on your device. The Turkish translation is included in the app.

Some additional surahs in the same recitation are downloaded from our storage at Supabase (server region: eu-west-1 (Ireland, EU)) the first time you play them and then kept on your device. The hafiz recordings for the prayer texts are included in the app; nothing is downloaded for them.

With each request, the service concerned receives technically necessary data such as your IP address and which file is requested, that is, which surah or verse. No identifier, name or prayer data is transmitted. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in providing the content you open, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

10. Mosques nearby

On iPhone, the app searches for nearby mosques through Apple's map service (MapKit), but only when you open the search. For this, a search term (for example "Mosque") and the place saved in the app, as the centre of a search area of about 10 km, are sent to Apple, not to us. The route opens in Apple Maps.

Where this list is not available, for example on Android, the app opens your device's map app with the search term and place. That app then processes the data under its own privacy policy.

11. Anonymous usage counters

To improve onboarding, the Premium screen and Prayer Focus, Ebru only counts on our server (Supabase, server region: eu-west-1 (Ireland, EU)) how often per day

  • an onboarding step was shown and onboarding was completed,
  • onboarding suggested the plan for praying regularly instead of the learning path,
  • the Premium screen was opened (with where from) or a purchase was started,
  • the Prayer Focus hint was shown, tapped or accepted.

Only the day, the event, where it came from and the count are stored. There is no identifier, nothing is stored on your device for this, and no individual answers, content, prayer or learning data are transmitted. Our function uses the IP address only briefly in memory to block too many requests and does not store it. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in an understandable app, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

13. No ad tracking

Ebru contains no advertising, tracking or analytics SDKs and does not build advertising profiles. The only evaluations are the crash reports (section 8) and the anonymous counters (section 11). This website also uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising services.

14. This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc., USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address. Fonts and images are served from this same site. If you choose a language at the top, only your browser remembers that choice (local storage, no cookie); it is not sent to us. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

15. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent. Write to support@ebruapp.com. You can also complain to a data protection authority, for example the Bavarian State Office for Data Protection Supervision (BayLDA).

16. Children and teenagers

The Friends feature is intended for people aged 16 and over. Younger users need their parents' consent.

17. Changes

If the app changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
